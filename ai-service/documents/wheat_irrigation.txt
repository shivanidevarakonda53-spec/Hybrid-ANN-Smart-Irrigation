# Wheat Crop Irrigation Knowledge

## 1. Introduction
Wheat is an important cereal crop. It requires suitable soil moisture at different growth stages for healthy development and good grain production.

## 2. Important Irrigation Stages

### Crown Root Initiation (CRI)
This is one of the most important irrigation stages. Water availability supports proper root development.

### Tillering Stage
Suitable soil moisture supports the development of additional shoots.

### Jointing Stage
Water is required for stem development and crop growth.

### Flowering Stage
Avoid water stress because it may affect grain formation.

### Grain Filling Stage
Adequate moisture supports grain development and improves grain quality.

## 3. Factors Affecting Water Requirements
- Soil type and water-holding capacity.
- Temperature and weather conditions.
- Rainfall availability.
- Crop growth stage.
- Irrigation method.

## 4. Water Management
- Irrigate according to soil moisture and crop requirements.
- Consider rainfall before irrigation.
- Avoid excessive watering.
- Maintain proper drainage.
- Use efficient irrigation methods where suitable.

## 5. Benefits of Smart Irrigation
Smart irrigation helps farmers identify suitable irrigation times, reduce unnecessary water usage, and manage available water efficiently.
